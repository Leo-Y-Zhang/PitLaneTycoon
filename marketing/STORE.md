**Name:** Pit Lane Tycoon

**Description:**

Run a racing team's factory and build it into a champion. Presses stamp out car parts, the
conveyor carries them through the paint shop and the wind tunnel to scrutineering, and every
part that passes earns cash for the next machine. Your garage builds itself as you grow, from a
bare plot to floodlights, a pit wall and a glass-fronted upper deck.

Hire a pit crew from Common to Legendary, chase rare Gold and Rainbow parts, and join a live
server event every 12 minutes. Between purchases, take your team's car out and drive your own
race car on the time-trial track: the fastest laps go on the board at the spawn. Win the
Constructors' trophy, start a new season and earn more every time, with the Reverse GP from
season 2 and the Endurance circuit from season 3.
